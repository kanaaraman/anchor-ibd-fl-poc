01 · The problem

Each registry alone is too small

Sweden and Norway have comparable healthcare systems and strong IBD registries, but research stays national. Cohorts are too small for treatment-response and subgroup studies, and patient data cannot simply be pooled across the border under GDPR, national law and the EHDS.

04 · How a round works

One round in four steps

What NVIDIA FLARE’s swarm workflow does on the three Sparks, every round.

STEP 1

Train locally

Each hospital trains the shared model for a few epochs on its own patients, on its own Spark’s GPU.

STEP 2

Send to the averager

One hospital is picked at random. The others send it their weights, encrypted for that hospital only.

STEP 3

Average

The averaging hospital weights each update by its number of training steps and combines them.

STEP 4

Share and repeat

The new model goes back to every hospital. Each keeps the best one, by validation loss.

05 · How the Sparks handle it

One Spark per hospital, not a cluster

Each Spark is an independent site inside its hospital’s network. A small coordinator host only admits sites and starts jobs. Hover the layers.

11 · Rollout on Sparks

    
From one Spark to three hospitals

    

      
1 · IDENTITIES
Certificates
RISE creates one kit per participant and sends each hospital only its own.
nvflare provision -p deploy/project.yml

      
2 · COORDINATOR
Any small host
Admits sites and starts jobs. Ports 8002 and 8003 open here only.
deploy/run_site.sh server <kit>

      
3 · EACH SPARK
Hospital client
Build the image, check the GPU, mount the OMOP extract read-only.
deploy/run_site.sh client <kit> <data> <ip>

      
4 · SUBMIT
Start training
The admin submits the swarm job to the deployment.
job.py --workflow swarm --env prod

    

    

      
HardwareOne DGX Spark per hospital, one small Linux host as coordinator

      
NetworkDNS name for the coordinator; hospitals need outbound TCP 8002 only

      
SoftwareDGX OS with Docker, NVIDIA PyTorch container from NGC, NVIDIA FLARE 2.9

      
DataAn OMOP CDM extract per hospital; real data needs ethics approval, a DPIA and data agreements

      
PeopleAn IT contact at each hospital and one admin at RISE
